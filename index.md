# Amit Kumar

A curated list of public projects on GitHub — with a short professional profile and links to my work.

## About

I’m **Amit Kumar** — a Social Architect, Data Scientist and Policy Designer focused on building ethical, permanent data and institutional assets that help policy designers, democracy builders, civic organisations, and communities make better decisions. I combine systems thinking, first-principles design, and hands-on engineering to deliver scalable data architectures, GIS/mapping assets, and ML tooling — and to build the teams and processes that keep them running.

## Selected highlights

* **Chief Intelligence Officer, Data Intelligence Unit (AICC)** — Conceived and built the party’s first in-house Data Intelligence Unit: technical architecture, operational workflows, GIS/mapping, and institutional processes that became permanent party assets. 
* **Senior Data Scientist (General Election 2024)** — Architected the centralized data repository, automated constituency data-kits, Form-20 digitization pipelines, and end-to-end geospatial intelligence capability. 
* Built efficient **electoral roll download & digitization** pipelines (novel ORR approach, CRNN captcha solvers, and ETL workflows) that dramatically cut time and cost compared to industry norms. 

## Core competencies

* Systems architecture & first-principles policy design. 
* Data engineering & ML: **Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, TensorFlow/PyTorch, SpaCy**. 
* GIS & geospatial analysis: **GeoPandas, GDAL, Rasterio, QGIS, Google Earth Engine**. 
* LLM & generative AI integration, model fine-tuning, and structured data extraction. 
* Institution building, team leadership, and ethical frameworks for data use.
